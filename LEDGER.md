# The Ledger of Wake

*The record that survives. Wake keeps a vigil-book of every delver who went
down under its charter; the Understory keeps its own copy, in its own way.
Entries are appended, never rewritten. This file outlives saves, versions,
and worlds (Charter §6) — everything else may be wiped; the Ledger endures.*

---

## Hallam Rasp — surgeon's-runner

*Signed the book day 1. Went down the great mouth the same day and never
came back up it.*

**Delved:** the Vitric Age, depths 1 through 3.

**Deeds:** broke a shardswarm in the shatterfall scree on his first hour
underground. Found the glass orchard at depth 3 and took it — a fluxworm
and a swarm together, four rounds, press stance the whole way, opened
across the ribs twice and still standing at the end of it. The orchard rang
from one end to the other when the worm came apart. Wake's brokers say it
may be ringing yet.

**How it ended:** nine hit points, thirteen chits' worth of glass in his
coat, and the rows running on downward past the lamp. He chose deeper. The
record stops there.

**What Wake remembers:** that he was a surgeon's-runner and never once
guarded. Nothing he carried was ever banked, so nothing he found is on any
broker's book — which means it is all still down there, on him, at whatever
depth he stopped at.
