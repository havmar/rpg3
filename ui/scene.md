# Day 1 — the shatterfall scree

**Isolt Tallow, surgeons-runner, signed the book this morning. She is at
depth 1, and a cullet crab is between her and everything else.**

The keeper of the book didn't ask why. She wrote the name on the page after
Teodor Slake's — third name in the new book, first one still breathing —
and said the assay-house has a standing order out: **unmelted ledger-clasps,
paying double** today.

The scree is a slope of crushed glass, loud underfoot, faintly moving. The
crab has armored itself in slag and shard and is unhurried about closing
the distance: it has correctly identified that you cannot cross the slope
quietly, and it is simply waiting where the sound comes down.

**The crab:** hp 9, armored — soak 3 against your knife's 1d6+1.

**Your footing:** hp 17/17, grit 3, light 10, supply 3, satchel 0/7,
4 windings on the drum.

  +1 guard, leaves when badly hurt)
